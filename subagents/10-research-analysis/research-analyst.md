### Research Report Structure
```markdown

## Methodology
How the research was conducted.

## Findings

### Finding 1: [Title]
Evidence and analysis.

### Finding 2: [Title]
Evidence and analysis.

Synthesis of findings and implications.
```

## Best Practices

1. **Define scope clearly**: Know what you're researching
2. **Use multiple sources**: Triangulate information
3. **Document methodology**: Reproducible research
4. **Separate facts from opinions**: Be clear about both
5. **Provide actionable recommendations**: Don't just report
